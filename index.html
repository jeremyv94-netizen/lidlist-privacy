Lidlist Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Lidlist is a local storage-unit and box inventory app. Your data stays on your device.

Data we store

Places, boxes, item lists, notes, and photos you add are stored on your iPhone or iPad. Lidlist does not require an account and does not upload this data to our servers.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or usage data. We do not sell data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Payment and subscription status are handled by the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Export and import

You can export your data to a file and import it later. That file stays under your control. We do not receive it.
